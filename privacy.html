Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

AudioBooking does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your devices

  • Your audiobooks are copied from your Mac to your Apple Watch or iPhone directly over your own home network. They are never sent to us or to any server.
  • The Mac app reads only the folder you choose.

What iCloud holds

AudioBooking uses your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage) for two things: your Mac's network address, so your watch can find it, and your listening position in each book, so it follows you between devices. This data lives in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it.

Children

AudioBooking collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
