AudioBooking for Apple Watch, iPhone and Mac

Getting started

  1. Install AudioBooking on your Mac and choose the folder that holds your audiobooks.
  2. Open AudioBooking on your Apple Watch (or iPhone). It finds your Mac through iCloud: use the same Apple ID on both.
  3. Tap a book to download it. Keep the watch on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac while it downloads.

Which files work

M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF and CAF. A folder of MP3 files becomes one book with one chapter per file. Copy-protected Audible files (AAX) cannot be played.

The watch cannot find my Mac

  • Check that AudioBooking is running on the Mac (crown icon in the menu bar).
  • Check that the Mac and the watch are on the same Wi-Fi network and the same iCloud account.
  • Allow AudioBooking to use the local network when asked.
